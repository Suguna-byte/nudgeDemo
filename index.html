Common questions
Does it hurt?

Most people feel a quick pinch. Babies usually settle within a minute. Cartilage feels stronger but passes in a second.

Why not just go to the jeweller?

Reusable piercing guns can't be fully sterilised between customers, and a blunt stud can crush tissue. Our studs and needles are sealed and used once.

What age can my baby get pierced?

From 3 months, after a short health check. For pre-term babies, our paediatric advisor reviews first.

Do you do belly, lip, eyebrow and tongue?

Yes, for clients 18 and over, all with a sterile single-use needle and implant-grade titanium starter jewellery.

Do you pierce noses?

Yes. Nostril and septum, with a sterile needle and a titanium or gold-plated starter piece.

Can you come on our muhurtam time?

Yes. Share the date and time when you book. Early mornings included.

Not sure which piercing?

Send a photo of your ear on WhatsApp. A nurse will suggest a placement.
